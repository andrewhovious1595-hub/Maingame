OUR PROCESS

Clear scope. Traceable inputs. Purposeful visuals.

A disciplined workflow helps everyone understand what the exhibit is intended to show—and what it does not establish.

  1. 01

    Initial case review

    Discuss the incident type, audience, intended use, timeline, and visual question. No confidential evidence should be sent through the public inquiry form.

  2. 02

    Scope and materials inventory

    Define deliverables, source materials, required inputs, assumptions, exclusions, review responsibilities, and project schedule in writing.

  3. 03

    Source review and organization

    Catalog supplied items and record relevant references. Identify missing, conflicting, or unverified information for client review.

  4. 04

    Visual development

    Prepare the agreed diagrams, annotations, timelines, or animations. Visual conventions should distinguish source facts from assumptions and illustrative elements.

  5. 05

    Review and revisions

    Provide a review draft, collect consolidated feedback, and document revisions within the agreed scope.

  6. 06

    Final delivery

    Deliver the agreed formats with appropriate notes, source references, limitations, and version information.
