SERVICES

Visual tools for complex incident information.

Project scope is tailored to the matter, available source material, intended use, and review requirements.

Vehicle collision exhibits

Illustrative roadway and scene diagrams, vehicle position sequences, travel-path graphics, impact-area annotations, and event timelines based on supplied materials and an agreed scope.

  • Scene and roadway overview diagrams
  • Vehicle position and movement illustrations
  • Annotated photographs and evidence callouts
  • Chronology and sequence exhibits

Premises incident exhibits

Visual organization of premises layouts, relevant locations, documented conditions, and movement paths.

  • Floor-plan and site-layout exhibits
  • Hazard and condition callouts
  • Photograph annotations
  • Location and event sequence diagrams

Workplace incident exhibits

Visual summaries of work areas, equipment locations, activity sequences, and supplied incident documentation.

  • Worksite overview diagrams
  • Equipment and location labeling
  • Event chronology
  • Annotated source-image exhibits

Pedestrian and roadway user exhibits

Illustrative diagrams of crossings, paths, scene context, and supplied visibility-related information. Any technical sight-distance analysis must be separately scoped and supported by appropriate data and expertise.

  • Crosswalk and roadway context
  • Pedestrian and vehicle path illustrations
  • Scene orientation and evidence markers
  • Event sequence diagrams
Important distinction

Visual exhibit preparation is not automatically forensic reconstruction. Engineering calculations, collision-dynamics analysis, causation opinions, expert testimony, or other specialized opinions are only provided when separately engaged and performed by appropriately qualified professionals.
